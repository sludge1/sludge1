## What I work on

- **Scientific software:** translating models, spreadsheets, research, and expert knowledge into maintainable code.
- **Agricultural intelligence:** soil and tissue interpretation, nutrient prioritization, crop and environmental modeling, and carbon-intensity workflows.
- **Applied AI:** combining structured knowledge, deterministic calculations, retrieval, and language models to create explainable decision support.

## Selected work

### LPI Product Positioning System

Engineering work for Nutrien Ag Solutions on an agronomic decision-support prototype combining deterministic rules, structured agronomic knowledge, DRIS nutrient-balance calculations, case retrieval, and AI-generated explanations. The work includes soil and tissue interpretation, validation interfaces, and an architecture that separates agronomic decisions from their explanations.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **LPI is proprietary client work and is not available publicly.** Its source code and project materials are not offered through this GitHub profile. This description is a high-level summary of my professional experience.

### Carbon-intensity software

Calculator and API integration work connecting agricultural management data with GREET/FDCIC workflows. My contributions include data schemas, management-practice mappings, calculation workflows, and ongoing integration updates.

### Agricultural Magic

Developing product strategy and architecture for agricultural intelligence services that give customers control of their data and knowledge. Areas of exploration include field-level decision support, source provenance, model validation, farm organization, and practical AI-assisted workflows.

This is an evolving development effort; the scope described here includes research and design work.

As an **Entrepreneur in Residence** and creator of the **Viability Accelerator**, I help founders connect customer discovery, product iteration, pricing, and founder-led sales. I work with teams on the evidence needed to continue, change direction, or stop.
